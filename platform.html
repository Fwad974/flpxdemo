01 · Connect

One connected foundation for all your data.

Data, apps and teams in one secure, governed layer.

  • Unified data layer

    Records, files and lab data in one model.

  • Single sign-on and roles

    One identity; each person sees only what they need.

  • Real-time sync

    Systems stay in step, with full change history.

  • Shared workspaces

    Teams and partners work on the same data.

02 · Integrate

Integrate your LIMS and your own apps.

Keep the systems that work. Connect them through a LIMS connector and open APIs.

  • LIMS connector

    Samples in, results back, with no manual transfers.

  • Open APIs and webhooks

    REST and events for your own applications.

  • Standard formats

    FASTQ, BAM, VCF, CSV and JSON.

  • Integration monitoring

    Live status, alerts and automatic retries.

How it works

  2. Configure
  3. Validate
  4. Operate
