Already have a VCF? Start at interpretation.

Upload the annotated VCF and the normalised PASS VCF it was made from. Interpretation runs on its own: no alignment, no variant calling.

  • Annotated VCF + normalised PASS VCF → the interpretation reports
  • Add the final BAM and its filtered VCF → the sample-entry checks and the clinical report chain
  • Add a GVCF → the ancestry report
  • No .bai? It is built for you.
  • Anything not uploaded drops the reports that need it; the run tells you which and why.
