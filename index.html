What runs online today: the Human WGS pipeline.

Five stages. Stop at any of them and download what that stage produced.

  1. 01

    FASTQ

    Your raw reads, R1 and R2.

  2. 02

    BAM

    Reads aligned to the reference genome.

  3. 03

    VCF

    Variants called from the alignment.

  4. 04

    Annotated VCF

    Variants annotated against current references.

  5. 05

    Interpretation

    Findings with their evidence, for clinical review. Not a diagnosis.

Runs on a Flopexa pipeline service. Without one connected, the page previews the flow and uploads nothing.

The platform

Five AI modules on one data layer.

Flopexa Genomics OS is one platform: five AI modules — Research, Therapeutics, Stem Cell, Veterinary and Clinical — running on the Flopexa Cloud, a single data layer. Each module reads the same genomic data at a different level, from raw reads to the person or animal in front of you. Pick the one that matches your work.

Underneath all five modules

One data layer.

Your genomic data lands once in the Flopexa Cloud and every module analyses it in place. Nothing is copied or exported between modules, so access, audit and retention are set once. Your results are returned to you.

Land

Data lands encrypted, in region.

Normalise

Each file is converted once into one schema.

Govern

Access, audit and retention are set centrally, once.

Serve

Analyses run where the data lives, so working copies are not scattered across tools. Results come back to you.
