What it does

How it works

Reads in

FASTQ, one sample per horse or camel.

Map

To the species’ own reference, not a human proxy.

Screen

Variants that matter for the species and breed.

Report

Written for the treating vet, with the evidence.

You need: sequencing reads (FASTQ) for the animal. You get: a report for the treating vet with hereditary, parentage or performance findings.
