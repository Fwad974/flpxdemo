Clinical AI

How it works

Receive

The VCF is placed against the sample’s existing record.

Annotate

Variants annotated against current references.

Match

To literature, guidelines and therapies; ACMG secondary findings only with consent.

Assemble

A sourced report, for the clinical team to sign off.

You need: an annotated VCF and the normalised PASS VCF it came from; optionally the final BAM, filtered VCF and GVCF. You get: a sourced variant report with therapy matches, held for review.

Scope

Built for: clinical genetics and MDT teams who sign off variant reports.

Not for: direct-to-patient results.
