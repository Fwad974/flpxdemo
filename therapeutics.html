What it does

Therapeutics AI

What it does

  • Target identification

    Ranks candidate targets from genomic and pathway evidence, showing the evidence behind each.

  • Biomarker discovery

    Finds markers that separate likely responders from the rest, from the same data.

  • Lead optimisation

    Models activity, selectivity and off-target behaviour of candidate structures.

Also: mechanism modelling, off-target assessment, patient stratification, translational readouts.

Therapeutics AI

How it works

Four steps, one run. Nothing is re-uploaded between them and nothing leaves the platform.

Define the target

Candidate targets are ranked from the genomic and pathway evidence in your data.

Screen candidates

Candidate structures are scored against known binding behaviour for the target.

Model the effect

Activity, selectivity and off-target behaviour are modelled for each candidate.

Stratify

Markers that separate likely responders are proposed from the same data, for the trial design to test.

You need: annotated variants: VCF files from the Human WGS pipeline, or your own, plus pathway or expression results where you have them. You get: ranked targets, candidate biomarkers and a responder-population summary, each with its sources.

Scope

Built for: discovery and translational teams working from genomic and pathway evidence.

Not for: patient-facing reporting; for that, see Clinical AI.
