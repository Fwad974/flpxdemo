Therapeutics AI

What it does

  • Target identification

    Candidate targets ranked, with the evidence behind each.

  • Biomarker discovery

    Markers that separate likely responders from the rest.

  • Lead optimisation

    Activity, selectivity and off-target behaviour, modelled.

Also: mechanism modelling, patient stratification, translational readouts.

Therapeutics AI

How it works

Define the target

Targets ranked from the genomic and pathway evidence.

Screen candidates

Structures scored against known binding for the target.

Model the effect

Activity, selectivity and off-target effects, per candidate.

Stratify

Responder markers proposed, for the trial design to test.

You need: annotated VCFs (from the Human WGS pipeline or your own), plus pathway or expression data if you have it. You get: ranked targets, candidate biomarkers and a responder summary, with sources.

Scope

Built for: discovery and translational teams.

Not for: patient-facing reports; see Clinical AI.
